## 4. What is Lean Management?
Lean Management is a systematic approach to improving a process by maximizing customer value and reducing activities that do not add value.

## 5. What is 5S?
5S is a workplace organization method: Sort, Set in Order, Shine, Standardize and Sustain.

## 6. What is Kaizen?
Kaizen means continuous improvement through small, regular improvements involving the people who work with the process.

## 7. What is Six Sigma?
Six Sigma is a data-driven approach for reducing defects and process variation. A common framework is DMAIC: Define, Measure, Analyze, Improve and Control.

## 8. Explain DMAIC.
Define the problem; Measure relevant data; Analyze causes and patterns; Improve the process; Control the improved process through standardization and monitoring.

## 9. What is a bottleneck?
A bottleneck is a process step that limits overall flow or capacity, often creating queues or restricting throughput.

## 10. What is root-cause analysis?
It is a structured attempt to identify the underlying reason for a problem rather than only treating its visible symptom.

## 11. Explain 5 Why.
Ask “Why?” repeatedly to move from the visible symptom toward an underlying cause. Five is a guideline, not a fixed number.

## 12. What is a Fishbone diagram?
A Fishbone/Ishikawa diagram organizes possible causes of a problem into categories such as Man, Machine, Method, Material, Measurement and Environment.

## 13. What is Pareto analysis?
Pareto analysis ranks problems or causes by contribution so a team can prioritize major contributors first.

## 14. Productivity vs efficiency?
Productivity generally relates output to input. Efficiency compares performance with a target or standard. The exact formula should be defined for the process being studied.

## 15. How would you improve high defects?
Define the defect, collect and segment defect data, use Pareto analysis, investigate causes with 5 Why/Fishbone, implement corrective action, then monitor the defect rate.

## 16. How would you identify a bottleneck from data?
Compare cycle time, waiting time, queues, utilization and output across process steps. A step with consistently high cycle time or queue buildup is a candidate for bottleneck investigation.

## 17. How would you use Excel?
For cleaning, filtering, formulas, Pivot Tables, KPI calculations, charts, comparisons and management reports.
